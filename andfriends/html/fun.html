The Joy Factor :

&FUN is life in full color.

Workshops that spark creativity you didn't know lived inside you. Community events that birth friendships in real-time.

Game nights, film screenings, dance parties, and moments of pure shared joy. This is where intention meets spontaneity. Where structure gives way to flow. Where people remember why showing up in person still matters.

What We Do :

&The Programme

  1. Learn something new from people who live it. These sessions aren't lectures, they're collaborative explorations where everyone leaves with fresh skills and fresh friendships.

  2. The kind of nights where competition and camaraderie dance together. Where strangers discover they're actually kindred spirits.

  3. Documentaries that ignite conversation. Indie films that shift perspective. Screenings followed by discussions that dive deep. Cinema as communal experience.

  4. DJ sets curated with intention. Dance floors that welcome all levels. From groove-focused sessions to high-energy electronic nights, the rhythm adapts to the community.

  5. Sometimes it's simple. Casual meetups that grow into something bigger. The unstructured moments often matter most.

It's Contagious :

Laughter and camaraderie is the soundtrack played loud and proud here. The kind that comes from deep within. The kind that connects strangers and turns them into collaborators, confidants, sometimes partners for life.

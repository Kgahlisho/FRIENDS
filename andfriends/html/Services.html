What We Offer :

Think of us as your creative partner, not just a rental. We're equipped to host intimate experiences or larger gatherings. Artists launching exhibitions. Entrepreneurs facilitating strategy sessions. Musicians performing for devoted listeners. Thought leaders hosting masterclasses. Filmmakers screening their work. The space adapts.

What matters is intention. If your event strengthens community, sparks creativity, or builds connection—we're the right venue.


Venue Details :

5 De Beer isn't a sterile event space. It's lived-in, warm, and full of character. Original wooden beams. Ambient lighting that shifts with mood. A bar that flows naturally. Sound system ready for acoustic sets or DJ performances. Flexible layouts for everything from intimate dinners to standing-room gatherings.

Maximum capacity: 250 people (flexible based on configuration)

Schedule a space tour (virtual or in-person). Discuss layouts. Ask questions. Get a feel for how your event fits into 5 De Beer.

Investment Level :

Our pricing varies significantly based on event type, date, time, guest count, and technical requirements. We pride ourselves on transparent pricing and flexible options.

Weekday Bookings: Often more affordable. Ideal for workshops, screenings, intimate events.

Weekend Bookings: Premium rates reflect demand. Perfect for higher-energy celebrations and larger gatherings.

Off-Peak Periods: Special rates available. Negotiate directly for longer-term bookings or recurring events.
